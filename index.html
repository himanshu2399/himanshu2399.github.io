About

I'm a DevOps & Cloud Engineer focused on leveraging tools like GitLab, Jenkins, Terraform, and Kubernetes to streamline cloud operations. Across roles at Onix, Health Catalyst, Searce, and Cognizant, I've built expertise in CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes management, and cloud infrastructure provisioning — mostly on Google Cloud.

I care about simplifying complex systems and making environments robust, automated, and efficient. Whether it's redefining infrastructure to manage petabytes of data, migrating a healthcare platform from on-prem to GCP, or hardening the deployment pipeline for a national bank, I aim to improve reliability and the day-to-day experience of the developers who depend on it.
